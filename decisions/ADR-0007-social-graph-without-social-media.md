# ADR-0007 — Social graph without social media

## Accepted initial-release timing amendment — 2026-09-26

[ADR-0025](ADR-0025-initial-mvp-scope.md) defers friend-informed ranking and friend-attendance context from initial release. Existing source-authorized current roster access remains; no new public attendance-history projection is authorized. People text discovery, friendships and consent-based DMs remain required. The accepted anti-social-media/privacy principles remain unchanged; richer discovery is post-launch scope.
Status: Accepted  
Date: 2026-09-21

## Decision
Support people discovery, DM requests, friendships, friend-informed ranking, and public attendance for public hangouts. Do not support posts, likes, comments, followers, public friend counts, or popularity scores. Friendship exists to improve real-world coordination.
