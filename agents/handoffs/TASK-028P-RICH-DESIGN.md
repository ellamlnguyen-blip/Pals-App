Lock-order review correction: the existing `set_people_preference` obtains the actor account `FOR UPDATE` first. Pilot management takes social `(16016,1)`, then exclusive pilot evidence `(16027,1)`, then policy/account rows; adding either advisory lock after the existing account lock would create a cycle. The proposed migration must replace the People RPC and use social → shared pilot evidence → policy/capability → People/rich gates → actor account → opt-in-only Auth/membership/campus → People/rich preference rows for **both** consent writers, including opt-out. No profile/Storage row is locked after the account; text publishability is freshly checked without claiming direct-profile-write serialization. Closed/missing gates and lost eligibility cannot prevent active-owner opt-out. Concurrent People-off/rich-on and policy changes are resolved by the shared order and fresh post-wait checks; external Auth/status deadlock aborts remain retryable failures.
