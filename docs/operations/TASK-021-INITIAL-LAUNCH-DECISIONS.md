## Still Proposed: owner inputs that scope acceptance cannot supply

Recommend retaining the current narrow report-only moderation workflow for hosted planning. Its local acceptance does not accept hosted staffing, MFA, retention or delivered-photo exposure. Before any hosted moderation migration or live use, the following responsible functions must supply concrete values and approve the resulting policy package. These are responsibilities to assign, not named people or promises of available coverage.

| Required responsibility | Exact input to record and review |
| --- | --- |
| Product/safety operations owner | Named accountable operators and backup coverage; who triages/reviews cases and handles uncovered periods; chosen response expectations and user-facing wording; escalation/contact routes for urgent issues, operator-target/conflicted reports and appeals; authority and handoff between those routes. Do not represent reporting as a staffed emergency service without an accepted, evidenced commitment. |
| Security/operator-access owner | Who approves, performs and independently verifies initial role bootstrap, assignment, revocation and offboarding; MFA requirements/enforcement evidence; account/device compromise handling; lost-factor recovery approver and verification method; recovery/bootstrap audit evidence and test plan. Recovery must not rely on the conflicted operator approving their own access or grant a broad reader. Any change to current authority needs its own proposed ADR. |
| Data/privacy owner, with safety and release owners | Per-data-class schedule: purpose, access/export authority, retention trigger and duration, deletion versus redaction/anonymization, legal-hold initiator/approver/release, exceptions, enforcement mechanism and verification evidence. Cover Auth/identity/profile/photos, Hangouts/membership/private instructions, friendships/DM generations/text/retry evidence, Hangout chat, notifications/preferences, private attendance, reports/provenance/cases/sanctions/immutable audits, backups/logs and any private size signals actually found in the target. Identify how access loss/block/sanction differs from deletion, how exports avoid third-party private data, and how backups/restores preserve holds and deletion decisions. No blanket cascade, irreversible deletion policy or numeric period is selected by this document. |
| Photo-delivery security/privacy owner | Hosted signed-URL lifetime and allowed creation/sharing paths, cache behavior/headers and measured CDN exposure, copy describing bearer access, incident contact/escalation and handling of a leaked/preissued URL. Decide whether surviving bearer access is acceptable for hosted release or a separately designed immediate-revocation mechanism is required; state how evidence/holds are preserved. Verify sanctions deny new authenticated app/Storage reads and new URL issuance, and separately measure a preissued URL through expiry and caches. |

[ADR-0021](../../decisions/ADR-0021-preissued-photo-url-revocation-boundary.md) accepts only a disposable-local boundary: committed sanctions deny new authenticated photo access, while preissued bearer URLs survive until encoded expiry; hosted caching may extend exposure. Owner-only app photo delivery is uncached, but it does not cancel a separately issued bearer capability. A short recommended lifetime would still require an explicit value, enforceable issuance control and hosted evidence; this package selects none. Do not delete evidence, rotate shared signing material or broaden service-role access as a shortcut. Local full resets are not a hosted retention policy.
