# Competitor / Reference Notes

## NomadTable
Reference for map-first activity discovery, pins, opening an activity from the map, joining, and coordinating with participants.

Pals differs through university verification, campus density, friendship, people discovery, calendar, persistent campus social graph, and stronger student/offline-safety assumptions.

Monitor campus-event tools, friend-making apps, local-activity marketplaces, coordination tools, and university social networks. Research informs decisions but does not silently overwrite accepted principles.
