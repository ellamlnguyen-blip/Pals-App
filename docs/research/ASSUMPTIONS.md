# Assumptions to Validate
- students will post casual plans publicly enough to create supply
- students will join plans with people they do not know
- friend attendance improves join conversion
- map discovery is more compelling than a conventional feed
- verified real identity improves trust enough to justify signup friction
- lightweight creation produces more supply than formal event creation
- UNC can reach enough density for the map to feel alive
- Supabase Realtime is sufficient for MVP chat
- Mapbox supports desired experience/cost
- Vercel + Supabase are sufficient for public UNC launch

Each assumption should eventually link to evidence, an experiment, or a metric.
