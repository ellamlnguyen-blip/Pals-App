# Problem

College campuses contain thousands of potential friends and activities, but social opportunities are constrained by dorms, classes, clubs, teams, formal programming, and existing circles.

Students routinely think “anyone want dinner?”, “I want to play pickleball”, “what is happening tonight?”, or “I want to meet people outside my normal group.” Today those intentions usually require already knowing whom to ask.

The opportunity is a verified campus network where user-created plans become discoverable while retaining the informality of a text like “anyone want tacos?”
