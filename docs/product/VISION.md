# Product Vision

Make it normal for a college student to open Pals, see what people around campus are doing, and join or create something without needing a club, formal event, or pre-existing friend group.

## Core Promise
**Make a plan. Find a plan. Meet people through doing something together.**

## Growth Loop
Create hangout → discovery → join → coordinate → meet offline → form friendships → easier future plans → more hangouts.

## Success
Pals succeeds when students create plans they otherwise would have kept in existing group chats, join plans with people they did not previously know, attend offline, repeat, and form future social connections.
