# Product Principles
1. Real-world activity is the outcome.
2. A hangout should be easier to create than a formal event.
3. Meeting through an activity is less awkward than browsing strangers without context.
4. People discovery is useful, but hangouts remain the center of gravity.
5. Friendship is coordination infrastructure, not public status.
6. Do not add social-media mechanics by default.
7. Map exploration should feel playful, not invasive.
8. Public location may be approximate; private exact details can be gated.
9. Safety features are launch features.
10. Do not reward scale/popularity for its own sake.
11. Organizations may exist later but should not turn Pals into a promotion board.
12. New features should make a real hangout easier to create, discover, coordinate, or safely attend.
