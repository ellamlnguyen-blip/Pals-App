# Users

## Initial Market
Verified students at UNC Chapel Hill.

## Primary User Types
- **Initiator:** has an idea, needs fast creation/invites and loose details.
- **Explorer:** wants something to do; needs map/time discovery and low-friction joining.
- **Connector:** brings people together; needs co-hosts, visibility/eligibility, reliable chat.
- **Expanding-circle student:** wants people beyond existing circles; needs verification, expressive profiles, interest discovery, DM requests, friendship.

## Not Primary for MVP
Businesses, non-students, mass-event promoters, organization accounts, cross-campus visitors.
